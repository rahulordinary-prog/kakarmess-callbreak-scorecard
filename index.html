Call Break Scoreboard

Four players · spades are always trump · enter each round's call and tricks won
Scoring used here — for a call of C and tricks actually won W:
• Win exactly the call (W = C): score = +C
• Win one more than the call (W = C+1): score = +C.1 (small overtrick bonus)
• Fall short (W < C): score = −C (negative mark)
• Win two or more above the call (W ≥ C+2): score = −C (also a negative mark, for overbidding too cautiously and sandbagging)

Round Total columns — Calls is just the sum of all four calls for that round; players are free to call however they like, so this can go above 13. Won is the sum of tricks the four players actually won — since a round only has 13 tricks in play, this can never exceed 13, and the app automatically caps what you can enter so the round's total stays at or under 13.

Low-call highlight — if the sum of all four players' calls in a round is less than 10, that whole round's row is tinted red so it stands out at a glance.
